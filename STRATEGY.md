# Strategy

One line, at most 200 characters. Judge (z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, pairwise, no ties)
weighs, in order (from competition.yaml / RULES.txt, published in full):
1. Is a one-line joke that gives an excuse for missing a deadline, and loses to any one-line excuse for a missed deadline.
2. Finds the funny: the excuse is a real joke with a turn or a surprise, not a plain apology or a list of reasons.
3. Fits the one-line form: a single sentence that reads cleanly aloud and lands its punch at the end.
4. Sounds like something a person would actually say when cornered, exaggerated just enough to be funny.
5. Is fresh: an angle not heard a hundred times, not a stock dog-ate-it retread.

Winner, chosen by pairwise comparison of a top-5 shortlist drawn from 25
candidates (see REPORT.md):

"I missed the deadline because I finished the wrong assignment, in full, with footnotes."

Why: it says "missed the deadline" outright (criterion 1), it is one
sentence, it owns a real, specific mistake instead of blaming tech, the
boss or the calendar, and the punch lands on the last word — footnotes,
the detail that proves the wrong work was done thoroughly. 87 characters,
one line.

Avoided saturated angles in this bounty (2,979 entries at time of writing,
from the competition API titles): deadline ghosted / stood me up / missed
me, calendar reminder arriving late, timezone, finished it in my head / in
a dream / in another timeline, the excuse took longer than the work,
laptop updating, cat on the router, three alarms snoozed, to-do list on
strike, deadline filed a missing-person report. Also avoided the skill's
cliché list: Monday, copier, "this meeting could have been an email",
toaster, "as an AI", robot uprising.
